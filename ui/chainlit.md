# Welcome to DBAgent 🗃️

Your AI-powered database assistant. Ask questions about your data in natural language!

## Getting Started

1. **Select a database** from the dropdown in settings (⚙️)
2. **Ask a question** like "How many customers do we have?" or "Show me the top 5 products by sales"
3. **View the results** - see the generated SQL, execution details, and visualizations

## Example Questions

- "How many orders were placed last month?"
- "What are the top 10 customers by revenue?"
- "Show me a breakdown of sales by category"
- "Which products have low inventory?"

## Tips

- Use **follow-up questions** to refine your queries
- Click **Steps** to see how your question was processed
- Charts are generated automatically when appropriate
